> **Local environment note.** `openpyxl`, `pandas`, `XlsxWriter` and
> `markitdown` are installed (markitdown may not be on PATH; run it via
> `python -m markitdown`). `scripts/recalc.py` works with LibreOffice using
> the patched `office/soffice.py`. LibreOffice CLI: `soffice.com` on Windows
> (`%ProgramFiles%\LibreOffice\program\soffice.com`), `soffice` on macOS/Linux.
