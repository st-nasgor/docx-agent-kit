> **Local environment note.** Node is typically not installed, so
> `pptxgenjs` and `scripts/apply_theme.js` are unavailable — create decks
> with `python-pptx` (installed) or by unpacking a template and editing
> `ppt/slides/slideN.xml` directly. The Python scripts (`slide_text.py`,
> `thumbnail.py`, `add_slide.py`, `clean.py`, `office/validate.py`) work.
> LibreOffice CLI: `soffice.com` on Windows
> (`%ProgramFiles%\LibreOffice\program\soffice.com`), `soffice` on macOS/Linux.
